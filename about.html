About Us

Learn more about BiteBuddy and our mission to deliver happiness

Who We Are

BiteBuddy is a modern food delivery platform that connects hungry customers with the best local restaurants. Founded with a simple belief that everyone deserves access to great food, we've built a seamless ordering experience that brings restaurant-quality meals right to your doorstep.

Our platform partners with over 500 restaurants across multiple cities, offering everything from traditional Indian cuisine to international flavors. Whether you're craving a classic Chicken Biryani or a gourmet Margherita Pizza, BiteBuddy has you covered.
